---
name: Editorial review governance
description: Scope and preservation rules for periodic reference-library reviews.
---

Shared-library findings require explicit global editorial authorization, never company-owner/member authority. Retain historical role-based attribution honestly; new named responsibility must refer to an actual authorized human. Preserve initial findings and append complete snapshots rather than overwriting history. Schedule/assignment-only changes must not imply another source review.

**Why:** The library is shared by all companies; company ownership does not establish authority to change shared legal findings. In-app editorial work replaces code-only maintenance without broadening company privileges or pretending that scheduling verifies a source.

**How to apply:** Keep editorial provisioning separate and operator-controlled; do not automatically grant the first company owner access. Make editorial work usable without company membership. Later reviews append dated complete findings with primary evidence and bounded claims; scheduling retains the last review date/status. Review reminders use Asia/Jakarta calendar days and become overdue only after the scheduled day. Consume the same current findings in the library and new AI context; do not rewrite old consultation records.
