---
name: Editorial review governance
description: Scope and preservation rules for periodic reference-library reviews.
---

Shared-library findings are managed through code-reviewed editorial updates, not company-member edits. Editorial owners are accountable roles, not invented claims that a named person reviewed a source. Preserve the initial findings and append complete snapshots for later reviews rather than overwriting history.

**Why:** The existing library is shared by all companies; company ownership does not establish authority to change shared legal findings. Periodic-review work must preserve earlier evidence without pretending to repeat initial verification.

**How to apply:** Future editorial updates must append a dated snapshot, including unchanged findings when appropriate. Introducing an in-app editor requires an explicit editorial authorization model. Review reminders use calendar days in Asia/Jakarta, become overdue only after the scheduled day, and never automatically change verification status.
